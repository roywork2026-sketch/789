# Safeguarding and safety policy (template for 789)

1. WE ARE NOT A CRISIS, LEGAL OR MEDICAL SERVICE. We give general information and point to free help. We say this on every screen and in the first text.
2. IN DANGER: always show 999 first. For mental health crisis: NHS 111 (mental health option, England), Samaritans 116 123, Shout (text SHOUT to 85258). Check these on nhs.uk every 3 months.
3. IF SOMEONE TELLS A VOLUNTEER OR ADMIN THEY ARE AT RISK: stay calm, listen, do not promise secrecy, encourage them to call 999 or 111 or go to A&E, and offer to call with them. Write down only what is needed. Tell the named safeguarding lead the same day.
4. NAMED LEAD: [name, phone, email]. Deputy: [name].
5. NO PERSONAL DATA. The app and text service do not ask for names, status, documents or health details. Do not ask users for them. Do not store texts.
6. NO ADVICE ON CASES. Never tell anyone what to claim, whether they will win, or what to say to the Home Office. Refer to Citizens Advice, legal aid, or an OISC-registered adviser or solicitor.
7. NO MONEY FROM VULNERABLE PEOPLE for advice. Never take payment for immigration advice unless regulated to give it.
8. ADS AND PARTNERS: only regulated, checked organisations. Label sponsors clearly. No payday lenders, no unregulated advisers, no gambling.
9. VOLUNTEERS WHO TALK TO USERS: get enhanced DBS checks where the law requires them, give training, and never meet users alone.
10. COMPLAINTS AND MISTAKES: publish a contact email, answer within 5 working days, correct errors quickly.
11. REVIEW: translations and content are reviewed by native speakers and a regulated adviser or clinician before release, and after every major change.
